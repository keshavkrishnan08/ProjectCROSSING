Pedestrian Safety Intelligence
SafeDanger

This turns street video into a live read on pedestrian safety. It is not just a box around each object. It follows every person and every vehicle and works out who is in danger.

The goal

For each person we estimate the chance of being hit. Green means safe. Red means danger. The number is the model output and not a guess.

How it reads the scene

A detector finds people and vehicles in every frame. A tracker gives each one a stable identity and measures speed and heading. From the size of each box the model recovers real world depth.

Why depth matters

A car can look close on screen and still be far away. The camera angle fools the eye. The model corrects for this so a distant car does not raise a false alarm.

When risk goes up

Risk climbs only when a moving vehicle is aimed at a person and closing in and near. An orderly crossing where the cars wait stays green. A car bearing down on someone in its path turns red.

Trust the number

The score is calibrated against labeled outcomes. A value near eighty really does mean that about eighty in a hundred such moments become a near miss.
